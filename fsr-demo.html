Hardware readout demo

16 x 16 FSR Array: DMUX to ADC to MCU

A row-select demultiplexer energizes one FSR row at a time. The 16 columns form voltage dividers through 10 kOhm pull-down resistors, the ADC samples all columns, and the MCU receives the frame over SPI.
